Toddler Nest

A Bridge from Home to School

Ages 18 months – 3 years

Our toddler environment is a warm, reassuring bridge from home to school. Meticulously prepared by AMI-certified guides, the space allows toddlers to develop physical coordination, vocabulary, and practical life skills like washing hands, watering plants, and preparing simple snacks at their own pace.

Fine and gross motor coordination

Rich language-immersion environment

Practical life and personal independence

Mon – Fri  ·  8:30 AM – 12:30 PM

The Curriculum

Key Developmental Areas

Practical Life

Fostering independence through real-world activities. Toddlers learn to dress themselves, prepare snacks, water plants, and care for their environment.

Language Acquisition

A rich language-immersion environment filled with conversation, songs, poetry, and precise vocabulary naming materials to support explosive language development.

Psycho-Sensory Motor

Activities designed to refine both gross and fine motor skills. From walking on the line to grasping small wooden cylinders, children develop crucial coordination.

Daily Rhythm

A Typical Day in the Nest

Our daily rhythm is slow, peaceful, and entirely guided by the child's needs. We do not rush. Instead, we allow ample uninterrupted time for deep concentration and joyful discovery.

08:30 AM

Arrival & Morning Work

Children independently hang their coats, change into indoor shoes, and choose their first activities of the day.

10:00 AM

Community Snack

A communal time where children help prepare organic snacks, set the table, and practice grace and courtesy.

11:00 AM

Outdoor Nature Play

Exploration in our secure nature garden, building gross motor skills and a deep connection to the earth.

12:00 PM

Story Circle & Dismissal

We gather for songs, quiet reading, and a peaceful transition before parents arrive for half-day pickup.

Nurturing curiosity, independence, and a lifelong love of learning.
