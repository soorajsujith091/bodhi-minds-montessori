Thriving Garden

The Heart of Montessori

Ages 3 – 6 years

The core primary classroom is the heart of Montessori education. Within a mixed-age setting, children work with sensory and manipulative materials that make abstract mathematics, grammar, botany, and geography concrete. Older children consolidate knowledge by mentoring younger peers, building profound social responsibility.

Advanced sensorial math and geometry

Phonetic reading and writing readiness

Mixed-age mentoring relationships

Mon – Fri  ·  8:30 AM – 3:00 PM

The Curriculum

Key Developmental Areas

Sensorial Math

Mathematics comes alive through beautifully crafted beads, boards, and cubes, making abstract mathematical operations remarkably tangible and deeply understood.

Phonics & Literacy

Using sandpaper letters and a movable alphabet, children trace, sound out, and eventually write and read words naturally and joyfully, guided by their own readiness.

Social Mentoring

Our mixed-age classroom creates a vibrant community where older children cement their own learning by mentoring younger peers, fostering deep empathy and leadership.

Daily Rhythm

A Typical Day in the Garden

Our extended three-hour work cycles allow children the profound luxury of deep, uninterrupted concentration. Their days are balanced with outdoor exploration, communal dining, and social connection.

08:30 AM

Morning Work Cycle

An uninterrupted 3-hour period where children independently select lessons ranging from botany to mathematics.

11:30 AM

Outdoor Learning & Play

Free play, gross motor activities, and hands-on tending of our organic school garden.

12:30 PM

Community Lunch

Children set tables with real dishware, serve one another, and engage in polite dinner table conversation.

01:30 PM

Afternoon Work Cycle

Continued exploration of complex materials for older children, and quiet resting time for our youngest peers.

Nurturing curiosity, independence, and a lifelong love of learning.
