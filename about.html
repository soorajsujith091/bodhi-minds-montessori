About us.

"We don't teach children — we create the conditions for them to teach themselves."

Bodhi Minds began as a dream shared by two educators who believed that childhood deserves more — more wonder, more freedom, more trust. In 2015, with a single classroom and twelve curious minds, we opened our doors in Bengaluru.

Today, we serve over 200 families across three programs, guided by internationally trained AMI educators. Our approach honors the child as a capable, self-directed learner who thrives in a carefully prepared environment.

Every space, material, and interaction at Bodhi Minds is designed to support the natural unfolding of each child's potential.

Our philosophy.

Follow the child

Every child has an inner guide. We observe, listen, and prepare the environment so each child can follow their natural developmental path with confidence.

Prepared environment

Our classrooms are meticulously designed — beautiful, ordered, and child-sized — inviting exploration and fostering independence at every turn.

Whole child education

We nurture not just academics, but social-emotional growth, physical coordination, creativity, and a deep connection to nature and community.
