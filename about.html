Our Story

About Us

Home > About Us

"We don't teach children — we create the conditions for them to teach themselves."

Bodhi Minds began as a dream shared by two educators who believed childhood deserves more — more wonder, more freedom, more trust. In 2015, with a single prepared classroom and twelve curious minds, we opened our doors to serve families seeking authentic Montessori guidance.

Today, we serve over 200 families at our beautiful Oakville campus, guided by internationally trained AMI educators. Our approach honors the child as a capable, self-directed learner who thrives in prepared environments.

Every space, material, and interaction at Bodhi Minds is designed to support the natural unfolding of each child's potential.

How We Guide

Our Core Philosophy

Follow the Child

Every child has an inner guide. We observe, listen, and prepare the environment so each child can follow their natural developmental path with confidence.

Prepared Environment

Our classrooms are meticulously designed — beautiful, ordered, and child-sized — inviting exploration and fostering independence at every turn.

Whole Child Focus

We nurture not just academic learning, but social-emotional growth, physical coordination, creativity, and a deep connection to nature and community.

What We Stand For

Our Core Educational Values

01

Dignity & Respect

We treat every child as a full, capable human being deserving of respect, and support them in making meaningful independent choices.

02

Cultivating Independence

Through structured practical life activities and accessible shelves, we help children learn to do for themselves and trust their own abilities.

03

Nature-Integrated Learning

We believe the outdoors is a vital classroom. Organic gardens, nature exploration, and play are fundamental parts of our children's daily rhythms.

Nurturing curiosity, independence, and a lifelong love of learning.
