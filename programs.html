Ages 18 months – 3 years

Toddler Nest

Our toddler environment is a warm, reassuring bridge from home to school. Meticulously prepared by AMI-certified guides, the space allows toddlers to develop physical coordination, vocabulary, and practical life skills like washing hands, watering plants, and preparing simple snacks at their own pace.

✓Fine and gross motor coordination
✓Rich language-immersion environment
✓Practical life and personal independence
🕒 Mon – Fri  ·  8:30 AM – 12:30 PM

Ages 3 – 6 years

Thriving Garden

The core primary classroom is the heart of Montessori education. Within a mixed-age setting, children work with sensory and manipulative materials that make abstract mathematics, grammar, botany, and geography concrete. Older children consolidate knowledge by mentoring younger peers, building profound social responsibility.

✓Advanced sensorial math and geometry
✓Phonetic reading and writing readiness
✓Mixed-age mentoring relationships
🕒 Mon – Fri  ·  8:30 AM – 3:00 PM
Enquire About Thriving Garden
Primary 6 – 9 years

Cosmic Education

At the elementary level, children are driven by a desire to understand the universe and their place in it. Through Cosmic Education, we present integrated lessons in history, biology, geology, and astronomy. This environment fosters collaborative research, creative writing, and advanced mathematical computations.

✓Integrated science and history curriculum
✓Collaborative group projects and research
✓Complex mathematical reasoning
🕒 Mon – Fri  ·  8:30 AM – 3:30 PM
