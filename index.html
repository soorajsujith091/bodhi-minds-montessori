A school that breathes at the child's pace.

At Bodhi Minds, we believe education begins with observation. Our AMI-certified guides follow each child's natural development, creating an environment where independence, concentration, and a love of learning flourish organically.

Founded on the principles of Dr. Maria Montessori, our school integrates nature, community, and purposeful work into every moment of the child's day.

Our Story

Our three paths of learning.

Each program is tailored to the developmental stage of the child.

T

Toddler community

18 months – 3 years

A gentle introduction to independence through practical life activities, sensory exploration, and language-rich interactions in a prepared environment.

Learn more →
P

Primary preschool

3 – 6 years

The heart of Montessori education. Children work across five curriculum areas: practical life, sensorial, mathematics, language, and cultural studies.

Learn more →
K

Kindergarten

5 – 6 years

The culminating year where children synthesize their learning, develop leadership skills, and prepare for their next educational journey with confidence.
