Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best age to start Montessori? +

Children can join our Toddler Nest environment starting from 18 months. The earlier a child joins, the more fully they benefit from the self-guided routine, but we welcome children at all program age ranges.

What does AMI certification mean? +

AMI (Association Montessori Internationale) is the official training standard established by Dr. Maria Montessori. All lead guides in our classrooms hold AMI diplomas, ensuring high-quality, authentic classroom environments.

How do children transition to traditional schools? +

Montessori alumni transition exceptionally well. The skills they develop — self-regulation, deep focus, collaborative problem solving, and intellectual curiosity — prepare them to excel in any classroom environment.
