Frequently asked questions.

What is the best age to start Montessori?+

Children can join our Toddler Community from 18 months. The earlier a child begins in a Montessori environment, the more fully they experience the benefits of the three-year cycle. However, children are welcomed at any age within our program ranges.

What does AMI certification mean?+

AMI (Association Montessori Internationale) is the gold standard in Montessori training, established by Dr. Maria Montessori herself. All our lead guides hold AMI diplomas, ensuring authentic, high-quality Montessori education.

How do you handle transitions to traditional schools?+

Montessori-educated children are well-prepared for any educational setting. They develop strong executive function, self-regulation, and academic foundations. Our kindergarten year specifically focuses on building skills for a successful transition.

Can I visit the campus before applying?+

Absolutely! We encourage all prospective families to tour our campus. Visits are scheduled during school hours so you can observe classrooms in action. Contact us to book a tour.

Is financial aid available?+

We offer a limited number of need-based scholarships each year. Please reach out to our admissions office to learn more about financial assistance options and sibling discounts.
