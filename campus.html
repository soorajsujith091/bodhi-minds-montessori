The prepared classroom.

Every element in our classrooms is intentional — from the child-height shelves to the natural lighting. Materials are arranged beautifully and accessibly, inviting children to choose their work independently.

  • Child-sized furniture and shelving
  • Natural materials — wood, glass, ceramic
  • Floor-to-ceiling windows for natural light
  • Defined work areas for each curriculum

"Nature is not a place to visit. It is home."

Our outdoor spaces are as carefully prepared as our classrooms. Children climb, dig, plant, and explore in environments that challenge their bodies and spark their imaginations. No plastic — only natural, open-ended play structures.
